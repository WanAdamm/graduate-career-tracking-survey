The cohort gap, field by field

Current-job pay for the ~2010 and ~2018 cohorts. The earlier cohort is ahead in every field, from RM 1,930 in ICT to RM 31 in Services.

~2010 cohort ~2018 cohort

Premium is the ~2010 mean current salary minus the ~2018 mean, computed before rounding; it can differ from the displayed difference by RM 1. It mixes experience, age and period effects; it is not a causal return to experience.

What the pattern says

Field choice sets more than starting pay. First-job means span RM 419 across the ten fields; current-job means span RM 1,486.

ICT and Engineering turn modest starting differences into the widest current pay advantage; Services, Agriculture and Arts stay compressed. The policy lever is not only enrolment. It is also the pay ceiling of the sectors graduates enter.

Method in brief

Salary bands are converted to midpoints. The ranked sample keeps 2,112 of 2,296 paired respondents after removing 180 salary inversions; the General Programmes category (4 paired) is excluded. Education and cohort current-pay figures use all 4,800 valid responses. Full method and outlier audit.
