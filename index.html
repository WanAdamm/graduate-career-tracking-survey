🎯 Strategic Insights & Actions

Key policy and career implications derived from the empirical findings

🚀

High-Tech & Digital Premium

ICT and Engineering consistently outperform every discipline across starting salary, 10-year ceiling (RM 5,375), and wage growth rate (+163%). National education initiatives channeling capacity into digital infrastructure directly align with proven market rewards.

📈

Mid-Career Rebound in Business & Law

Business, Law, and Social Sciences graduates enter the job market with modest starting pay (~RM 1,800–RM 2,000), but demonstrate strong mid-to-senior acceleration, reaching RM 4,200+ by the 10-year mark. Early patience yields high compounding mobility.

⚠️

Breaking the Services & Arts Wage Trap

Graduates in Services, Arts & Humanities, and Agriculture face severe early and late stagnation, with average wages stuck near RM 3,000 even after a decade in the workforce. Targeted technical upskilling and transition pipelines into higher-value digital roles are urgently needed.

📋 Methodology & Data Integrity Notes

• Midpoint Imputation: Salary survey bands were mapped to representative numeric midpoints (e.g., First Job `RM 1,000 and below` = RM 750; Current Job `Below RM 1,500` = RM 1,250; `Above RM 15,000` = RM 17,500).

• Paired Wage Progression Metric: Calculated strictly for respondents who reported both First Job and Current Job salaries (n = 2,296): Wage Progression = Current Salary Midpoint - First Salary Midpoint.

• Filtered Clean Sample: Excluded 42 unvalidated records coded as 'Field unknown' or '99.0'. General Programmes (n=7) exhibited an anomalous negative progression due to high starting base outliers and small sample representation.
