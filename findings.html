Method and limitations

How the cleaned progression sample was built, and what it can and cannot say.

Method

Salary bands were converted to midpoints: current job from RM 1,250 (below RM 1,500) to RM 17,500 (above RM 15,000), and first job from RM 750 (RM 1,000 and below) to RM 12,500 (above RM 10,000).

Of 2,296 paired respondents, 180 reported a current salary below their first and were removed as reporting inconsistencies. The General Programmes category (4 cleaned pairs) was excluded from ranked comparisons, leaving 2,112 respondents across 10 fields.

Mean gain is current minus first midpoint. Mean growth is the average of individual percentage changes, not the ratio of mean salaries. Education and cohort current-salary figures use all 4,800 valid responses; first-job cross-sections use 4,467.

Limitations

  • Salary bands are coarse and top-coded, so means are approximations. Cross-sectional first-job means are sensitive to top-coded bands; Services includes 24 above RM 10,000 responses while its median is RM 1,500.
  • The removed inversions may include genuine pay cuts from career changes or part-time work. The estimates describe typical upward trajectories, not every individual outcome.
  • General Programmes remains in the raw counts but has too few paired responses for a reliable category mean.
  • Cohort differences mix age, experience and period effects. They are descriptive, not causal.
